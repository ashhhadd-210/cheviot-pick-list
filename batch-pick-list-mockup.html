Cheviot Trees Batch Pick List

Grouped using the raw Batch No, but displayed as a unique 6-digit Batch Group.

New raw files are merged into the existing pick list. Matching lines refresh without losing Status, Match Code or Notes; genuinely new lines are added; earlier lines remain; and archived batches are permanently protected. Pick-list Raw Frames is taken from raw data column B and calculated as B ÷ 32. The final Total Raw Frames column shows the total for the whole batch. Labels use the separate outward cell-size and correx calculation.

The outward frame total uses the quantity and cell size: Cell 3/4 = 200 plants per correx, Cell 5 = 150 and Cell 6 = 120. There are 10 correx per frame, and the result is rounded up to a whole outward frame.
